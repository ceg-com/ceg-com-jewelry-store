## 🧼 Jewelry Care Guide

Keep your favorite accessories looking beautiful with these simple recommendations:

- Store jewelry in a clean and dry place.
- Avoid unnecessary contact with perfumes, lotions, and harsh chemicals.
- Remove delicate accessories before swimming or showering.
- Gently clean jewelry using methods appropriate for its materials.
- Store individual pieces separately to reduce scratching.
- Review material-specific care instructions before cleaning.

**A little care helps preserve the beauty of your favorite pieces.**
